For four years at Plan to Perfection I led facade and envelope design on residential towers, private homes, commercial buildings and a healthcare high-rise, many of them for developer WECON. Across 20 projects, about 150,000 sq ft, 12 are built or under construction.

I also produced the firm's visualization and cinematic animations, and received the firm's Best Architect award for design excellence in 2024.
